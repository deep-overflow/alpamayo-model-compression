# One Loss Is Not Enough: Dual-Objective Structured Pruning of a Reasoning Vision–Language–Action Model

*Paper draft v0.2 — 2026-08-26. The paper's method is `dual` — unit selection guided
jointly by the action flow-matching (FM) loss and the reasoning cross-entropy (CE)
loss — and the central message is that both losses are necessary: either alone fails on
both channels. (v0.1 title — "What to Prune Matters More Than How Much" — kept as a
candidate subtitle.) Numbers are final (frozen 2026-08-19 protocol; every
open-loop number reproducible via `experiments/evaluation/paper_numbers.py`). Camera-ready
tables live in `reports/evaluation/2026-08-19_results_tables.tex` (Tables 1–7) and
`reports/evaluation/2026-08-11_baseline_table.tex`; this draft embeds condensed versions.*

---

## Abstract

Reasoning vision–language–action (VLA) models for driving are trained under two losses
in one network: a language cross-entropy (CE) loss supervises the VLM tower that writes
a chain-of-causation (CoC) rationale, and a flow-matching (FM) regression loss
supervises the action expert that denoises trajectories against the VLM's KV cache. We
argue — and demonstrate on Alpamayo-1.5-10B — that structured pruning of such a model
must be guided by **both** losses, and we make that the method: score every query head
and MLP channel by first-order Taylor importance under the FM loss and under the CE
loss of the model's own rollout (no human labels), and keep a unit if **either**
per-layer ranking wants it: `dual = max(rank I_FM, rank I_CE)`.

The necessity claim is causal, not aesthetic. The two capabilities occupy disjoint
substrates — different units, different layer bands (action: L0–17; language: L18–35),
different cache pathways — so each loss's gradient is structurally blind to part of the
network. At an exactly matched −24.0% budget this blindness is fatal in both
directions: FM-only pruning collapses language to a constant-answer floor (LingoQA
73.2 → 37.0) while not even being the best trajectory configuration, and CE-only
pruning fails to protect its own channel (degeneracy 14.2%) and drives significantly
worse than the unpruned model. Their rank-max union is synergy, not compromise: dual
beats both of its components on every evaluation set and both channels — measured over
7,599 clips per arm in open loop and 150 simulated scenes x 2 rollouts in closed loop —
and is the only −24% configuration that drives significantly *better* than the
unpruned model in closed loop (+0.079 scene score, p=1e-4; at-fault collisions 12 → 7),
where published one-shot baselines at the same budget (Wanda, Týr-the-Pruner) collapse
language below the floor. The criterion alone spans a 0.293 closed-loop range at fixed
budget — what you cut matters more than how much — while the standard proxies fail in
sign-reversing ways: open-loop minADE moves against closed-loop driving, and the arm
that preserves reasoning best collides 4.3x more. We complete the picture with the
largest hidden factor (the calibration set: a same-distribution redraw costs as much as
the pruning itself), six pre-registered negative results (score magnitudes, iterative
recalibration, second-order scores, allocation search, operator swaps, staged cutting —
nothing improves the one-shot dual recipe), and additive composition with 8-bit
quantization to 2.35x compression while still driving above the unpruned baseline.

## 1. Introduction

Driving VLAs are converging on a two-tower recipe: a large VLM consumes multi-camera
video and ego history, autoregressively writes a natural-language rationale
(chain-of-causation, CoC), and a smaller action expert decodes a trajectory by
flow-matching denoising against the VLM's KV cache. The reasoning text is not a debugging
artifact — it is a product requirement (interpretability, auditability) and, as we show,
a causal contributor to the action. These models are large (10B+ parameters) and
latency-sensitive, so compression is inevitable. But compression research for VLAs has so
far measured only the action channel (task success, action MSE); to our knowledge no
published VLA compression work evaluates what happens to the language/reasoning channel,
and none evaluates closed loop.

This paper turns that requirement into a method and a message: **to prune a reasoning
VLA, use both of its losses.** The model is trained under two objectives — the CE loss
of the reasoning text and the FM loss of the action — and we show that a unit-removal
signal derived from either one alone is structurally blind to part of the network, with
consequences on *both* channels. Our method, `dual`, scores every query head and MLP
channel under both losses and keeps a unit if either per-layer ranking wants it. We
validate the claim on Alpamayo-1.5-10B with a design built around three rules:

1. **Matched budgets.** Every criterion comparison removes *exactly* the same number of
   parameters (2,657,452,032; −24.0%) with the same per-layer allocation (19/32 query
   heads, 7,390/12,288 MLP channels in each of 36 layers), leaving the expert tower and
   the KV interface untouched. Differences are attributable to selection alone.
2. **Two channels, two loops.** Every headline arm is scored on trajectory accuracy
   (minADE/minFDE, open loop), driving quality and safety (scene score, collisions —
   closed loop in a full simulator), and reasoning (reference-CoC NLL, degeneracy rate,
   and LingoQA judged accuracy with a constant-answer floor).
3. **Pre-registration.** Decision gates are registered before each run; negative results
   are reported as first-class findings.

**Contributions.**

- **C1 — Method: dual-objective pruning, with necessity established by ablation.** We
  propose scoring units under both of the model's training losses (FM and CE) and
  keeping the per-layer rank-max union. At exactly matched budget, both halves are
  necessary: each single-loss criterion fails on *both* channels — including its own
  (FM-only is not the best trajectory config; CE-only degenerates its own language
  channel) — while the union beats both components everywhere and is the only −24% arm
  significantly above the *unpruned* model in closed loop (+0.079, p=1e-4). The
  operator matters only through its keep-if-either semantics (rank-sum ties max,
  rank-product fails), and the cost is one rollout plus one backward pass per
  calibration clip — no human labels.
- **C2 — Mechanism: why one loss cannot see the whole model.** Reasoning and action
  dissociate one-directionally across units, layer bands (action: L0–17; language:
  L18–35), and cache pathways (CoC tokens are 43x more causally dense per token than
  prompt text for the expert; 14 knockout routes damage language only, 0 damage action
  only). Each loss's gradient illuminates only its own substrate — e.g. layer 35 is
  structurally invisible to the FM loss, and FM-only selection there causes ~20x the
  language damage of any CE-informed criterion — which is exactly the blind spot the
  rank-max veto repairs.
- **C3 — A two-channel, two-loop evaluation protocol, and evidence that the standard
  proxies fail.** Open-loop minADE reverses sign against closed-loop driving in 3 of 5
  criterion arms (significantly so for the best one); a config that best imitates the
  human path (via layer-output reconstruction) *loses* to a plain one-shot cut in
  closed loop; and the arm with the healthiest reasoning channel collides 4.3x more.
  Neither "trajectory closeness" nor "reasoning health" can stand in for driving —
  both channels must be measured, in the loop.
- **C4 — What does not help, and what dominates instead.** Six pre-registered
  improvement attempts are rejected (score magnitudes, iterative recalibration,
  second-order scores, global allocation search, operator swaps, staged cutting); the
  largest remaining factor is the *calibration set* (swapping only the calibration
  images moves language capability by +35.2pp — 97% of the recoverable gap — and a
  same-distribution redraw costs as much as the 24% pruning itself). Published
  label-free baselines (Wanda, Týr-the-Pruner) collapse language below a
  constant-answer floor at the same budget; we localize Týr's failure to its
  prefill-only reconstruction Hessian — it actively hurts the decode path, and the
  model's own CoC at its natural 1.9% token share flips the sign.

Practical corollaries: pruning + uniform W8 quantization composes additively to 2.35x
(9.44 GB) while remaining significantly above the unpruned baseline in closed loop; and
knowledge-inheriting LoRA recovery erases criterion differences entirely at 33% pruning,
where the binding constraint becomes calibration/recovery *data diversity*, not the
selection rule.

## 2. Related Work

**Structured pruning.** Criterion-based structured pruning scores prunable units and
removes the lowest-ranked: gate-level Taylor importance (LLM-Pruner), activation-aware
weight magnitude (Wanda), and second-order layer-output reconstruction (OSSCAR), which
Týr-the-Pruner combines with a global sparsity-distribution search over a supernet. We
evaluate Wanda and Týr-the-Pruner at exactly matched budget and add the axes they do
not measure: generated-language quality and closed-loop control (Sec. 6.3). Closest to
our diagnosis, RAC (Chain-of-Thought reconstruction, arXiv:2509.12464) argues that
reconstruction calibration for reasoning LLMs must include on-policy CoT activations;
we port the idea to a VLA as a one-factor change of the reconstruction Hessian's token
set and confirm its mechanism in a setting where the CoC is only 1.9% of tokens yet
nearly orthogonal to the prefill activation subspace. All of these methods select (or
refit) units under a single objective; our claim is orthogonal to their machinery: in a
model trained under two losses, the selection signal itself must come from both.

**Vision–language–action models for driving.** Driving stacks are converging on VLM
backbones that couple language with planning — [cite: DriveVLM, EMMA, LMDrive,
DriveLM, ORION, ...] — and, most recently, on reasoning-centric driving VLAs such as
Alpamayo-R1 / Alpamayo 1.5, where the chain-of-causation rationale is an explicit
product output alongside the trajectory and the architecture is two-towered: a VLM
trained under a language CE loss and a flow-matching action expert reading the VLM's
KV cache. This two-loss structure is exactly what our method addresses, and Alpamayo
1.5 is our testbed. Language competence of driving models is benchmarked by LingoQA
(judged QA with Lingo-Judge); we adopt its protocol and add a constant-answer floor
calibration (answering "No." to every question scores 37.0), which turns out to be
load-bearing — several published-method arms land *below* it. To our knowledge no
prior work compresses a reasoning VLA while measuring both of its channels, in either
loop.

## 3. Model and Problem Setup

**Alpamayo 1.5 (10B).** Two transformer towers on a Qwen3-VL backbone:

| tower | hidden | Q heads | head_dim | MLP inter. | layers | params |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VLM text | 4096 | 32 | 128 | 12288 | 36 | ~6.9B |
| action expert | 2048 | 16 | 128 | 8256 | 36 | ~2.3B |

The VLM consumes 4 cameras x 4 frames plus ego-history trajectory tokens and generates
the CoC autoregressively; the expert runs 10 flow-matching denoise steps over 64
diffusion tokens, reading the VLM's per-layer KV cache as prefill, and decodes a 6.4s /
64-waypoint / 10Hz trajectory. Only `head_dim` (128) and the 8 KV groups match across
towers — that is the cache interface the expert reads, so **KV heads, k/v projections and
the cache are never pruned** (a hard interface). GQA coupling: KV group h feeds VLM Q
heads [4h,4h+4) and expert Q heads [2h,2h+2). The expert is 20.6% of parameters but only
5.5% of FLOPs and is step-bound rather than width-bound.

**What we prune.** Per-layer Q heads (removing q_proj rows / o_proj columns) and MLP
channels (gate/up rows / down columns), masked first (0/1 forward-pre-hooks; verified
functionally identical to removal) and then physically excised (`slim_lib`; uneven
Q-head counts per KV group require a gather-based attention forward, and the unpruned
model is benchmarked on the same code path for honest paired latency).

**Our method: dual-objective Taylor pruning.** The model is trained under two losses,
and the method uses exactly those two — nothing else — as pruning signals. All scores
are measured once, on the unpruned model, over a fixed 100-clip calibration set
(`calib_100`, drawn from official train, disjoint from every evaluation set):

- `I_FM` (the action half; `I_traj` / `traj` in tables) — first-order Taylor importance
  of multiplicative unit gates against the expert's trajectory flow-matching MSE,
  `mean_clips |∂L_FM/∂g_u|`, accumulated over the 10 denoise steps. Requires only
  ego-motion ground truth — free from driving logs.
- `I_CE` (the reasoning half; `I_CoC` / `coc` in tables) — the same Taylor score
  against the CE loss (NLL) of the model's **own rollout** CoC, teacher-forced.
  Requires a generation pass but *no human or curated labels* — the criterion is
  self-supervised on raw logs.
- The final score is a per-layer rank-max union:
  `dual(u) = max(rank_layer I_FM(u), rank_layer I_CE(u))` — keep a unit if *either*
  loss ranks it high. Rank normalization discards score magnitudes deliberately
  (Sec. 6.2 shows magnitudes have no stable target), and the max implements a veto:
  each objective can protect units the other cannot see.

**Ablations and variants.** `traj` and `coc` are the two halves used alone. `J` is a
label-free, rollout-free Jacobian-lens score (a logit-lens-style construction) — with
rows of the unembedding as
cotangents, one backward per dictionary token yields lens vectors at every layer, and a
unit's score is the size of its residual-stream write that is readable through the
unembedding, combined with activation second moments — used both alone and as
`jtraj = max(rank I_FM, rank J)`, the variant that asks whether the CE half's
*generation pass* is dispensable.

**Allocation.** Uniform across layers at ratio 0.3985632694 (matched to a legacy
budget; rounding to 0.40 would shift 17 MLP channels per layer and break exact matching).
Every criterion arm removes exactly 2,657,452,032 parameters (11.079B -> 8.421B, −24.0%).

## 4. Evaluation Protocol

**Open loop.** Three distribution-matched, pairwise-disjoint sets drawn from the
official splits by greedy weighted-L1 matching (weighted L1 ~ 0.0056 vs 0.085 random):
in-dist **val 500**, in-dist **test 500**, and **OOD 1,533** long-tail clips carrying a
curated reference CoC (with OOD-val 262 as the official-val subset used for headline
tables). Per clip: one CoC rollout (temp 0.6, top-p 0.98), K trajectory samples under it;
frozen protocol (2026-08-19) reports rollout-only minADE@6 / minFDE@6 **means** (medians
alongside; minADE is heavy-tailed). Seeds derive from clip identity
(`sha256(seed:clip_id)`), so every arm sees identical seeds and all comparisons are
clip-paired (bootstrap 95% CIs + Wilcoxon). Determinism is bit-exact within one GPU
architecture (all arms on RTX 5880 Ada); across architectures 3–4% of clips change their
generated CoC, so architectures are never mixed inside a comparison.

**Closed loop.** alpasim `public_2601`, 150 scenes x 2 rollouts per arm (300 rollouts),
scene-paired against the unpruned baseline. Power analysis fixed *before* reading
results: paired per-scene sigma ~ 0.32 gives a minimum detectable effect of 0.080 at 80%
power (N=30, used by an earlier matrix, resolves only 0.179 — the reason early matrices
were uniformly non-significant). Scene score is the gated progress score in [0,1]; we
also report pass rate, at-fault collisions, off-road, progress, distance-to-GT, and a
CoC degeneracy rate whose thresholds are aligned with the open-loop definition.

**Reasoning channel.** (i) NLL of the curated reference CoC on OOD (proper scoring
rule, same text for every arm); (ii) degeneracy rate (empty / token-soup heuristics);
(iii) **LingoQA** (500 questions, Lingo-Judge) via the model's native VQA interface,
zero-shot; and a **constant-answer floor**: answering "No." to every question scores
37.0 (51% of questions are yes/no), so any arm at or below 37.0 has no measurable
language capability. The unpruned model scores **73.2** — above the paper's best
zero-shot (GPT-4V 59.6, at 5 frames vs our 4). A CoC-probe track (frozen reader answers
from the generated CoC alone; blind floor 39.2 vs 62.4) measures how much scene
information survives *in the rationale itself*.

**Unpruned baseline (anchor).** Open loop val/test/OOD-val minADE@6 = 0.824 / 0.842 /
1.000 m (minFDE@6 2.205 / 2.267 / 2.496); closed loop scene score 0.750 [0.697, 0.801],
pass 89.0%, at-fault 0.040; CoC degeneracy 0.6% in both loops; LingoQA 73.2.

## 5. Results I: Both Losses Are Necessary — and Their Union Is Sufficient

*(Camera-ready: Table 2 in `2026-08-19_results_tables.tex`.)*

This section establishes the thesis by exact ablation: hold the budget fixed and vary
only the within-layer score — the FM half alone, the CE half alone, their union, and a
label-free replacement for the CE half.

**Matched-budget decomposition (−24.0% each; minADE@6 means; CL = closed-loop scene
score, Δ vs unpruned 0.750):**

| criterion | label-free | val 500 | test 500 | OOD-val | CL score (Δ) | CL degen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (baseline) | — | 0.824 | 0.842 | 1.000 | 0.750 | 0.6% |
| I_traj only | yes | 0.950 | 1.008 | 1.178 | 0.783 (+0.033) | 5.0% |
| I_CoC only | no | 1.558 | 1.584 | 1.546 | 0.660 (−0.089\*) | 2.0% |
| J only | yes | 1.942 | 2.158 | 2.557 | 0.536 (−0.214\*) | 1.4% |
| **dual = max(traj, CoC)** | no | **0.890** | **0.950** | **1.119** | **0.828 (+0.079\*)** | 2.7% |
| jtraj = max(traj, J) | yes | 0.937 | 1.028 | 1.175 | 0.791 (+0.042) | 0.8% |

Five arms, identical parameter counts, identical allocation, expert/KV untouched;
kept-set overlaps 62.7–75.7% — the criteria genuinely choose different units.

**Finding 1 — the criterion swings closed-loop driving by 0.293 at fixed budget.** From
−0.214 (J only) to +0.079 (dual), wider than the worst arm's gap to baseline (0.214).
"How much to compress" is the wrong question; the data only supports "what to keep."

**Finding 2 — each loss is necessary; the union is synergy, not a trade-off.** The
FM half alone fails on both channels: it collapses language to the constant-answer
floor (LingoQA 37.0; Sec. 6.1) *and* is not even the best trajectory configuration.
The CE half alone also fails on both: it degenerates the very channel it optimizes
(14.2% free-generation degeneracy vs 0.2% for J-only) and drives significantly below
the unpruned model. dual beats *both* of its components on every set (dual − traj
pooled in-dist: −0.061 [−0.101, −0.023]); the gap survives teacher-forcing the same
curated CoC into every arm (−0.108\*), so the CE term improves how the *trajectory*
units are selected, not just the text context — first-order FM-Taylor misses unit
interactions (the sub-additivity of Sec. 6.2), and the CE gradient, which flows through
computation shared with the action path, patches part of that blind spot.

**Finding 3 — the two failure modes are orthogonal.** J-only preserves the reasoning
channel *above* the unpruned model (reference-CoC NLL −0.019\*, CI below zero; CL
degeneracy 1.4%) while destroying trajectory (+187% open loop) — construct validity for
the J score, and direct evidence that reasoning and action live in different units.

**Finding 4 — open-loop minADE is not a driving proxy.** All three positive arms are
significantly *worse* than baseline open loop (+0.05 to +0.10 m median, p<1e-7) yet
drive at or above baseline closed loop. The rank order among arms is preserved across
loops, but the sign against baseline flips. Mechanism (measured, not assumed): pruning
improves precisely the scenes the baseline fails (baseline<1.0 scenes: 0.506 -> 0.718)
while slightly degrading already-perfect scenes, and roughly halves rollout-to-rollout
variance (repeat noise 0.145 -> 0.067) — a narrowed behavior distribution costs
imitation (minADE) and buys control.

**Finding 5 — reasoning health is not a safety proxy.** The healthiest-reasoning arm
(J-only) has 4.3x the at-fault collision rate; the highest-degeneracy positive arm
(traj-only, 5.0%) still drives above baseline. dual's entire closed-loop advantage is
one number: at-fault collisions 12 -> 7 (off-road unchanged).

**Label-free one-factor pair (dual vs jtraj; overlap 84.8%/83.3%).** Replacing the
reasoning half's rollout-based Taylor with the rollout-free J-lens costs +0.028 m
open-loop minADE (p=9.7e-8) and −0.037 scene score (p=0.032, borderline), and buys a
reasoning channel indistinguishable from baseline (reference-CoC NLL −0.031\* better
than dual; degeneracy 3.0%->1.6% open, 2.7%->0.8% closed). The pre-registered
substitution gate fails — the two criteria are *different*, not interchangeable — but
the direction of the trade is exactly what the J score's definition predicts, and it is
consistent across all three sets and both loops. No criterion in this paper uses human
labels; the axis that J removes is the *generation pass*, which matters because a
collapsed model contaminates its own CoC-NLL signal.

## 6. Results II: What Actually Moves the Numbers

### 6.1 The calibration set dominates the criterion (C3)

*(Table 3 in the .tex; LingoQA column.)* Single-criterion arms calibrated on AV clips
collapse LingoQA to the floor (traj 37.0, J 32.2, CoC 30.2 vs floor 37.0). Swapping
**only the calibration images** to the evaluation domain (LingoQA train; same CoC
objective, same budget) lifts CoC-only from 30.2 to 65.4 (+35.2pp — 97% of the gain);
additionally swapping the *objective* to VQA-answer NLL adds +1.0pp (n.s.). The
combined criterion is far more robust to calibration domain (dual 68.8 -> trajvqa 71.2,
+2.4pp n.s.) — the practical value of the rank-max union is partly *robustness to
calibration choice*. (Arms calibrated on LingoQA are no longer zero-shot and are
reported in a separate block.)

On the trajectory axis the same force is even blunter. With criterion, budget and
allocation fixed, changing only the 100 calibration clips (test-500 paired ΔminADE):

| design choice | cost (m) |
|---|---|
| calibrate on OOD clips | +0.191\* |
| **the 24% pruning itself** | +0.096 |
| redraw 100 clips, same distribution | +0.094\* |
| mix 100 OOD into 200 | +0.084\* |
| criterion choice (jtraj − dual) | +0.071 |

A same-distribution redraw costs as much as the compression itself and more than the
criterion. Every criterion comparison in this paper shares one fixed `calib_100` (so
paired conclusions stand), but absolute costs are extraction-dependent — a caveat we
believe generalizes to all calibration-based pruning papers. "Calibrate on the
evaluation distribution" is also rejected: OOD calibration is worse *on OOD too*
(+0.210\*), and half of its penalty is extraction luck.

### 6.2 Pre-registered negative results (C4)

*(Table 6 in the .tex.)* All gates registered before running:

| attempt | measured | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| use score magnitudes (loss-unit knapsack) | target (single-unit ablation damage) has split-half reliability ~0; 99–100% of units flip sign across clips | rejected — nothing stable to calibrate against |
| first-order additivity | group damage / sum of single damages = 1/5 … 1/55 (worse for larger groups) | broken at the operating point |
| staged recalibration (3 stages, re-scored on masked model) | +0.030\* test (+0.022\* val, +0.026\* OOD-val): **doubles** the one-shot cost; kept-set churn 10–11% | rejected — churn is perturbation, not adaptation |
| second-order score (diagonal empirical Fisher) | worse on all three sets, significant on two | rejected (predicted by the additivity diagnostic: the error is off-diagonal) |
| global allocation search on dual's ranking | +0.003 / +0.001 / −0.001, all n.s. (also with Týr's searched allocation transplanted) | no gain over uniform |
| combination operator | rank-sum ≡ max (−0.001 n.s.); rank-product +0.142\* | max kept; keep-if-either is the load-bearing property |
| ratio extension (u55 = 33%) | minADE 5.1x baseline, degeneracy 71% | one-shot knee is between 24% and 33% |

The through-line: unit-level Taylor scores are only meaningful as *ranks within a
composition*; every attempt to extract more (magnitudes, curvature, re-measurement,
searched budgets) either does nothing or hurts. One-shot `max(rank, rank)` at uniform
allocation remains the recipe. (An earlier 2x4 grid — criterion x allocation at fixed
budget — established the complementary fact: catastrophic CoC *collapse* is caused by
late-layer-heavy allocation (+0.500 main effect), not by the criterion (+0.165), while
the criterion governs graded NLL quality; "don't concentrate the budget late, use dual
within layers" summarizes both.)

### 6.3 External baselines fail on the axis nobody measures (C4)

Same budget, both label-free:

| method | test minADE@6 | LingoQA | CL score (Δ) | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wanda (|W|·||X||) | 2.975 | 9.2 (−27.8\* below floor) | not run | CoC degeneracy 87.6% |
| Týr uniform (OSSCAR recon.) | 0.999 | 20.8 (−16.2\* below floor) | not run | selection alone scores 2.2 |
| Týr (+ global search) | 0.950 | 34.2 (−2.8, at floor) | 0.786 (+0.037) | CL degen 5.9%, all empty output |
| dual (ours) | 0.950 | **68.8** (+31.8\*) | **0.828 (+0.079\*)** | one backward pass |

Týr ties dual on open-loop trajectory (all three CIs include 0) — its OSSCAR
reconstruction is genuinely effective on that axis, and its global sparsity search is
what buys the tie (removing it costs +0.006\*/+0.010\*/+0.016\*). But the reconstruction
leaves language indistinguishable from a model that answers "No." to everything, and in
closed loop Týr falls below dual (−0.042 [−0.085, −0.000]) with 61% more at-fault and
64% more total collisions while *winning* distance-to-GT-path — the open-loop metric's
axis — a second, independent sign that human-path imitation moves against driving
quality. Cost: 41 GB supernet + hours of search vs one backward pass.

**Why reconstruction kills language, and the 2-line fix.** Týr's reconstruction Hessian
is accumulated on the fused prompt *prefill* (93% vision tokens). Splitting the Hessian
by token stream and evaluating held-out per-layer reconstruction error shows the
prefill-fit refit **actively hurts the decode path** (+0.020/+0.035 vs no
reconstruction at the u40 point; CI excludes 0) while helping vision and prompt-text
paths — "buys trajectory, costs language" measured at layer level. The decode stream is
nearly orthogonal to the prefill activation subspace (only 0.117 of decode energy lies
in prefill's top-512 eigenspace for MLP), so adding the model's own CoC rollouts at
their **natural 1.92% token share** flips the sign of the decode error (88% of the full
effect); a matched-magnitude prompt-text upweighting control does nothing (30x smaller
effect), ruling out a conditioning explanation. Two auxiliary findings: the deployed
damping (1e-2) is not run-to-run reproducible (objective differs by 2.9%; the prefill
Hessian has effective rank ~13–46 of 4096–12288), and an equal-error re-allocation of
the same budget wants 70–83% cuts in early MLP blocks vs 5–13% mid-stack — uniform is
measurably suboptimal for reconstruction-based pruning even though searched allocations
buy nothing for selection-based pruning. The criterion lesson recurs here at the
activation level: a compression signal built from one stream cannot serve the path it
never saw — reconstruction needs the decode (CE-path) activations just as selection
needs the CE gradient.

### 6.4 Quantization: a criterion from the reasoning loss alone, and honest composition

Post-training fake-quant of the VLM pool (per-row bits, group-64 scales), bit allocation
driven **only** by the CoC reasoning loss (no trajectory label anywhere): at the W8
budget nothing separates (uniform ≡ guided ≡ baseline); at W4 the guided allocation
restores baseline-level driving on all three sets while uniform W4 degrades
significantly (guided − uniform: −0.009\*/−0.024\*/−0.015\*) — yet the guided allocation
*loses to uniform on the very metric that produced it* (reference-CoC NLL +0.0595,
p=1e-133), because the first-order sensitivity under-prices lm_head rows outside the
calibration support (2.3 effective bits). Teacher-forcing the same reference CoC leaves
the trajectory gain unchanged (−0.0152 vs −0.0151): the entire driving benefit flows
through the KV-cache numeric path, not the text — the expert reads the cache, not the
words. This is the quantization-side replica of the thesis: a budget allocated by one
channel's loss silently taxes the other channel (here, even the very channel that
supplied the signal), so a single-objective compression signal must always be paired
with measurement of the channel it ignores — in either direction.

**Composition** (test 500, paired): pruning and quantization damages are additive (the
per-clip interaction CI includes 0 in both cells), so the two stages can be tuned
independently and stacked:

| config | size (projected) | ratio | open Δ | CL score (Δ) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dual (prune only) | 16.84 GB | 1.32x | +0.108\* | 0.828 (+0.079\*) |
| **dual + W8 (expert incl.)** | **9.44 GB** | **2.35x** | +0.118\* | **0.813 (+0.064\*)** |
| dual + W4 (expert incl.) | 5.48 GB | 4.04x | +0.200\* | 0.768 (+0.018, n.s.) |

Uniform W8 over the whole model (expert included) costs +0.001 — below measurement
precision — and removes 9.95 GB, with the expert's share (2.18 GB) itself free — at the storage axis it dominates 24% structural pruning (which, however, is
the only lever that reduces FLOPs, and the only one that improves closed loop). The
2.35x arm is the recommended deployment point: significantly above the unpruned
baseline in closed loop. (Caveats: fake-quant storage is projected; quantization-only
closed-loop cells were not run.)

### 6.5 Recovery erases the criterion; data diversity is the lever

At the 33% knee (`u55`), zero-shot pruning collapses (minADE 4.26, degeneracy 71%) and
criterion differences are huge (traj vs dual up to +1.78 minADE). After
knowledge-inheriting LoRA recovery (KD from the unpruned model's CoC + trajectory loss),
the criterion difference collapses to ±0.05 (pooled −0.017 [−0.046, +0.011]; H0
accepted under a pre-registered gate): **recovery training erases the selection rule**.
Scaling recovery data 6.5x in *unique scenes* (1,200 -> 7,750) improves pooled minADE by
−0.063\* and makes 33%-pruned-plus-recovered beat 24%-pruned-zero-shot (−0.053\*,
p=0.026); an epoch-matched control on the small set gets *worse*, so the lever is scene
diversity, not less memorization per se. The mechanism is the language tower, not the
expert: held-out CoC CE drops 26% while the expert's flow-matching loss is unchanged
(−0.0009) — recovery improves the KV context the expert reads. Closed loop: recovered
u55 scores 0.823 (+0.074\*) with 0.000 degeneracy, statistically at the level of dual
u40. Implication: criterion anatomy (Sec. 5) matters for the zero-shot / cheap-recovery
regime; at higher ratios the research question becomes a data question.

## 7. Results III: Why One Loss Cannot See the Whole Model

Reasoning and action dissociate — one-directionally — in three independent
measurements, which together give the method its causal basis:

**Units.** The single-criterion arms (Sec. 5, Finding 3): J-only keeps reasoning at
baseline while trajectory collapses +187%; traj-only collapses LingoQA to the floor
(73.2 -> 37.0) while its minADE sits within 8% of the best pruned arm. If the two abilities shared units, neither
dissociation could exist. The unit-calibration study adds the microcosm: in layer 35
(structurally invisible to the trajectory loss), traj-selection becomes random w.r.t.
CoC and uniquely causes ~20x the CoC damage of any other criterion — the exact blind
spot the rank-max veto repairs.

**Cache pathways (attention knockout, expert side; protocol adapted from Map the
Flow, arXiv:2510.13251, extended to a two-readout setting).** Blocking the expert's view of
cache spans (text fixed by teacher-forcing, so only the pathway changes): the generated
CoC occupies a median 10 tokens and receives the *least* attention mass of any span
(2.3%), yet blocking it costs +0.134 m median (CI excludes 0) — 2.7x the cost of
blocking the entire 157-token prompt text, i.e. **43x more causally dense per token**
(16x vision). The attention sink takes 1.1% of mass and is causally exactly zero
(−0.0001). Attention mass is not importance, now with sign-reversing examples. Only the
front-telephoto camera is individually causal (+0.080\*; the other three are severally
redundant) despite ranking second-lowest in mass.

**VLM-internal edges (two-readout knockout).** Cutting information routes inside the
VLM and reading both channels: **14 cells damage language only; 0 cells damage action
only.** You can destroy the rationale completely (CoC<-instruction: NLL +582%) with the
trajectory unmoved, but never the reverse. Every action-significant band cell
lies in L0–17 (sink@L0–8, temporal cross-frame@L9–17); every language-significant band
beyond those two is L18–35; and whole-depth knockouts of the two action-critical routes
cost +64% (cross-frame) and +59% (sink). Same-camera *temporal* integration is
critical while cross-camera *spatial* integration is not significant. And the sink
dissociates by mechanism: causally empty as *content* (expert may ignore it) but
required as *infrastructure* (removing it inside the VLM costs −59%) — do not let a
token pruner near it.

Together these are the causal form of the thesis. Each loss's gradient illuminates
only its own substrate — the FM loss the early-layer, cache-numeric,
temporally-integrated action circuitry; the CE loss the late-layer decode path — so a
unit invisible to one loss can be critical to the other, and the rank-max union wins
precisely because each objective vetoes removals in the region the other cannot see.
The same anatomy explains why "reasoning health ≠ safety" is architecturally possible:
the expert consumes the cache *numerics* (which CE-selected units shape) rather than
the text — trajectory can improve while the rationale degrades, and vice versa.

## 8. Efficiency Accounting (honest)

Physical removal is verified equivalent to masking (fp64-exact per-layer; end-to-end
ΔminADE within noise). At −29.3% parameters: peak memory −29%, prefill −26%
(compute-bound), but end-to-end batch-1 latency only 1.03x vs a predicted 1.38x —
decode and expert denoising (66% of wall-clock) are kernel-dispatch-bound, so removing
29% of weights does not remove kernels. Width pruning buys memory, prefill, and
(composed with quantization) storage; it does not buy decode latency at batch 1 — that
axis belongs to step reduction / speculative decoding, which are orthogonal and already
published for this model family. We report this deliberately: several VLA compression
papers imply latency wins from parameter counts alone.

## 9. Limitations

- **One model, one simulator.** All results are on Alpamayo-1.5-10B and alpasim
  `public_2601` (150 scenes); the criterion anatomy is matched-budget at a single ratio
  (−24.0%) with uniform allocation, expert and KV untouched.
- **Closed-loop resolution.** N=150 resolves 0.080 at 80% power; arms inside that band
  (jtraj vs traj; dual+W4) are reported as indistinguishable. ~49% of scenes score 1.0
  for the baseline (ceiling), making the power estimate optimistic.
- **Quantization is fake-quant.** Storage numbers are projected from bit allocations;
  no packed kernels, no latency claim. Quantization-only closed-loop cells missing.
- **LingoQA caveats.** 4 frames vs the paper's 5; answers are 2.4x human length (a
  documented judge over-estimation regime; the concise-style control does not bind on
  this model); the floor calibration mitigates but does not remove this.
- **Calibration variance.** The +0.094 redraw effect is a single control draw;
  estimating extraction variance properly needs 3–5 same-distribution redraws.
- **Recovery confounds.** d5 trained on different hardware than its peer (accepted
  confound; effect 3x larger than measured cross-hardware noise); recovered-u55
  closed-loop for the scaled-data arm not yet run.
- **Determinism scope.** Bit-exact only within a GPU architecture; cross-architecture
  CoC text differs on 3–4% of clips (measured paired effect on minADE ~0).

---

## 10. Conclusion

Pruning a reasoning VLA is a two-objective problem, and the method must match: score
units under both of the model's training losses — the action FM loss and the reasoning
CE loss — and keep whatever either objective ranks essential. At a fixed −24% budget
the selection criterion spans a 0.293 closed-loop range, either loss alone fails on
both channels (including its own), and the rank-max union is the only configuration
that beats the unpruned model; the mechanism is a one-directional dissociation between
action (early-layer, cache-numeric, temporally-integrated) and language (late-layer,
decode-path) substrates that leaves any single-loss gradient structurally blind to
part of the network. The proxies the field uses — open-loop trajectory error,
reasoning-health heuristics, human-path imitation — all mispredict driving in
documented, sign-reversing ways, and the largest unmeasured factor in the pipeline is
the identity of the calibration set. We release protocols, pre-registered gates, and
negative results so the next compression paper on a reasoning VLA starts where this one
ends: prune with both losses, measure both channels, close the loop, fix the
calibration, and be suspicious of any improvement that was not pre-registered.
