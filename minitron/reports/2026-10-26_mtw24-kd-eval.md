# Experiment: Minitron-Width 24% + KD on Alpamayo 1.5 10B (`mtw24_kd`)

## Purpose
This run measures what the published Minitron recipe recovers on a driving VLA. The recipe is activation-importance
width pruning followed by logit KD, with the VLA adaptations D1–D11 from
`minitron/plans/2026-10-23_plan.md`. It is the baseline row for the new driving-VLA compression study.

The questions:
- Does KD close the zero-shot gap?
- How does the result compare with the dense model, and with MoOP at the same 24.0% budget? MoOP is zero-shot and
  untrained.

## Setup

**Model**
- Alpamayo 1.5 10B, revision `7aba8293`.
- MT-W prunes the VLM text tower only:
  - hidden 4096 → 3328, MLP 12288 → 9216.
  - Heads, KV and layers are kept.
  - The vision merger and DeepStack outputs are sliced with the same channels; the ViT is untouched.
  - 2,659,847,424 parameters are removed, which is 24.0% of the VLM.
  - Student size: 8,418,678,770 parameters.

**Importance**
- ModelOpt-style L2 activation importance: abs, then mean over positions, then square, then sum, then sqrt.
- Computed on `calib_1024` (1,024 KD windows).

**KD loss**
- Forward KL, τ = 1, full vocabulary, at the CoC + `cot_end` + `traj_future_start` + 128 GT discrete
  trajectory-token positions.
- Context: the teacher's CoC and GT trajectory tokens.
- Plus velocity MSE at GT interpolants, with t uniform on the inference grid.
- Knowledge insulation: stop-gradient at the KV cache. The two losses are summed plainly.
- Teacher targets are precomputed: full-vocabulary bf16 logits plus velocity per epoch slot, 413 GB.

**Data**
- `kd_r1`: one R1 window per clip, 9,905 windows, official train split.
  - 9,841 for training, 64 held out for loss monitoring.
- 3 epochs, about 95M tokens.

**Optimisation**
- AdamW (0.9, 0.95), weight decay 0.1.
- LR 1e-4 → 1e-5 cosine, warmup 40 steps.
- Global batch 64, 459 steps, full parameters, gradient clipping per tower.

**Hardware**
- cvlab21, 8 GPUs (Blackwell 0–3 + Ada 4–7).
- FSDP2 HSDP: sharded within each CPU socket, replicated across sockets. 2 samples/GPU × accumulation 4.
- 70.6 s/step, 9.19 h total.

**Selection and evaluation**
- Checkpoint selection: lowest mean minADE@6 on val500 among epochs 1–3. val500 is therefore not reported.
- Evaluation: `run_baseline.py` rollout protocol, k = 8, per-clip seed 42, max_gen 256, Ada 4–7.
  - minADE@6 / minFDE@6 is the minimum over the first 6 of 8 samples.
  - Comparisons are paired per clip, with 95% bootstrap CIs.
- Sets:
  - test_2000 (official test, distribution-matched, contains test500).
  - OOD-Val262.
  - LingoQA standard protocol: 500 questions, Lingo-Judge.
- Reference arms use the same protocol: dense, MoOP zero-shot (`slim_dual_u40_v2`) and MT-W zero-shot.

## Analysis (Opinion)

**KD repairs most of the zero-shot damage.**
- Recovery is about −0.42 m minADE@6 on test_2000 and −0.44 m on OOD-Val.
- CoC degeneration goes from 24% to 0.2%. Teacher-forced KD was enough to remove it, so the on-policy-KD
  motivation "CoC degeneration will remain" does not hold for this model.

**The recovered model is still clearly worse than dense (+0.115 m).**
- Trained, MT-W is slightly but significantly worse than MoOP without any training (+0.039 m).
- The gap is largest in the speed-change and turn buckets (decel_stop +0.082, turn +0.069) and smallest in cruise
  (+0.018).
- Cutting the residual (hidden) width cuts every input to every layer, text and vision alike, and a 95M-token KD
  does not restore that.
- The comparison is unfair to MT-W in one way: MoOP has not been retrained. MoOP + KD under the same recipe is the
  comparison that decides between the two pruning shapes.

**The LingoQA loss is a KD side effect, not lost reasoning.**
- About 88% of the KL positions are discrete trajectory tokens, so the student learned to answer "what is the
  current action" with a trajectory.
- The release masks that token range only while decoding CoC, and the LingoQA VQA path does not mask it.
- Excluding that one question type, MT-W + KD's VQA accuracy is near dense.
- Two ways to address it:
  - **Inference:** mask the trajectory-token range during text decoding. This is a cheap diagnostic to measure the
    leak exactly.
  - **Training:** mix VQA data into KD. Our dualr_wl result showed capability retention follows the data in the fit.

**Data scale is not ruled out as a limit.**
- val500 kept improving into epoch 3 (0.967 → 0.867), and the held-out KL fell in epochs 2–3.
- That improvement coincides with the LR decay, so the two causes cannot be separated from this one run.

**Suggested next steps**
1. MoOP + KD (and its importance re-measured on `calib_1024`) with the identical recipe, data and selection. This
   is the fair shape comparison.
2. LingoQA with the trajectory-token range masked, for every arm. It costs about 20 minutes per arm.
3. Latency and memory of the MT-W shape on one Ada card, against dense and MoOP.
4. The candidates in `minitron/plans/2026-10-25_vlm-pruning-kd-literature.md`, in order:
   1. on-policy distillation for the expert, using the student's own CoC as context
   2. VLM↔expert budget allocation
   3. KV-cache distillation
