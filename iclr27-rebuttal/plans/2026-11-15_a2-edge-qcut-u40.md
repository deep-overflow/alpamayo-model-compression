# Plan: Alpamayo 2 Edge u40 q-cut (fewer Q heads, more MLP channels) -- MoOP / FM-only / CE-only

2026-11-15. User ask: run the remaining driving lever, cutting fewer Q heads and more MLP channels at the same budget. On
A2 Super this (u50-q8) removed MoOP's driving loss.

User decisions:
- **q2 and q4 both.**
- **Test500 + OOD-Val250 + LingoQA.**
- Ada cards 4–7.

## Question

At u40 (6 of 16 Q heads per layer), MoOP drove no better than FM-only or CE-only, and l0keep did not change that.
- Does cutting fewer heads, with the same parameters taken from MLP channels, remove MoOP's driving gap?
- Does it keep MoOP's LingoQA edge over FM-only (+8.8 pp at u40)?

## Masks

`make_edge_moop_masks.py --q-cut 2 4` applies make_slim's `_qcut` rule with Edge's MLP cost:
- One Q head = 2 x 128 x 2048 = 524,288 parameters = 128 relu^2 channels (2 x 2048 each).
- So u40-qN cuts N heads and 3,673 + (6 − N) x 128 channels per layer:

  | variant | Q heads per layer | MLP channels per layer | removed |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | q2 | 2 | 4,185 | 509,329,408 |
  | q4 | 4 | 3,929 | 509,329,408 |

- q2 cuts 12.5 % of the heads, the share of A1.5's qcut4 (4/32) and A2's q8 (8/64).
- Gates (all passed):
  - G0: A1.5 regression including the shipped `slim_dual_u40_qcut4_v2`.
  - G0q: with 3 x hidden, the parameterised rule reproduces `make_a2_moop_masks` q-cut 4 on A1.5 for
    dual / traj / coc.
  - G0p: exact parameter parity with plain u40.
  - G0n: the cut heads are a subset of plain u40's, and the cut channels a superset.
- FM-only / CE-only overlap of the removed Q heads: q2 0.30, q4 0.38 (plain u40 0.51).

## Runs

- Six arms: `{moop,fm_only,ce_only}_q{2,4}`. Each on LingoQA, Test500 (2 shards) and OOD-Val250: 24 jobs.
