# Plan: Alpamayo 2 Super on LingoQA — dense vs MoOP / FM-only / CE-only (u40, then u50 and u50-q8)

Status: **done 2026-11-14 06:45 KST** -> report `iclr27-rebuttal/reports/2026-11-14_a2-moop-vs-halves.md`. Goal set by the user: "MoOP가 FM-only와 CE-only를 이길 때까지 해줘." (/goal).

## Purpose

- **Where A2 stands on driving (open loop).** MoOP never loses to CE-only. Against FM-only:
  - it ties at u40 on all three sets (test500 +0.007, OOD-Val +0.004, OOD-train −0.004 [−0.027, +0.019]);
  - it loses at u50 (+0.077*, test500 Blackwell).
- **Why the paper still prefers MoOP.** Its argument is that MoOP keeps the language/reasoning ability that the single criteria give up. A1.5 Table 4 (LingoQA, Ada):

  | arm | LingoQA accuracy |
  |---|---|
  | dense | 73.2 |
  | MoOP | 68.8 |
  | FM-only | 37.0 |
  | CE-only | 30.2 |

  So MoOP beats **both** halves there. A2's LingoQA has never been measured; it is the gap every A2 report lists.
- **Hypothesis H-L.** On A2, MoOP's LingoQA accuracy is higher than FM-only's and CE-only's. This is a paired, segment-bootstrap 95% CI that excludes 0, measured at the paper's recipe u40.
  - If true, A2 reproduces the paper's claim: MoOP ≥ each half on driving and > each half on language.
  - u50 and u50-q8 follow as secondary rows, because their masks exist.

## Protocol (the A2 twin of `experiments/lingoqa/run_lingo_vqa.py`)

- **Questions.** The 500 LingoQA val questions (100 segments, 2 human references each), the same manifest as A1.5 (`lingo_lib.load_manifest`).
- **Input.** Zero-shot, A2's native VQA task (`alpamayo2_super.text_tasks.build_text_task_messages(task="vqa")`): "Front camera:" with frames 0-3 as images, then the question verbatim.
  - The camera is index 1, "Front camera", the same as the A1.5 port.
  - Frames are the last 4 of the segment's 5.
  - The prompt was checked on CPU (11-13): system prompt, 4 frames, question; 750 tokens.
- **Decoding.** Greedy, max 128 new tokens, trajectory tokens masked (A2's own text-task logits processor). Truncations are tracked.
- **Masks.** The existing u40 / u50 / u50-q8 mask files, applied with the open-loop runner's pre-hooks.
- **Scoring.** `experiments/lingoqa/score_lingo_vqa.py` (Lingo-Judge, LingoQA's own rule) writes `scored.json`.
- **Statistics.** `ci_openloop_lingoqa.lingo_block`, as in the paper's Table 4 CI report:
  - accuracy with a 95% CI that resamples the 100 segments;
  - paired deltas vs MoOP and vs dense, 200k draws;
  - always-"yes" floor 37.6%.
- **Hardware.** cvlab21 Ada GPUs 4-7, one GPU type for the whole table. They become free when the u50/q8 open-loop run ends, ~07:30 KST 11-14.
  - **Changed 11-14:** the user allowed all 8 GPUs, so the whole table runs on Blackwell 0-3 instead.
- **Arms, in this order:** dense, MoOP u40, FM-only u40, CE-only u40, then the u50 three, then the u50-q8 three. Two pairs claim arms with mkdir.

## Gates

- **G-L1 (done, CPU).** The prompt matches A2's VQA template; 500 questions in 100 segments.
- **G-L2 (GPU, first pair, 3 questions).**
  - All-ones hooks give answers identical to no hooks.
  - MoOP masks change the answers.
  - Dense answers are non-empty and not truncated.
- **G-L3 (after scoring).** Dense A2 accuracy must be clearly above the always-yes floor (37.6%). Otherwise the protocol, not the model, is measured.

## Cost

- About 500 × (750-token prefill + ≤128-token greedy) per arm. On an Ada pair this is estimated at 25-40 min, unmeasured.
- 10 arms on 2 pairs is about 2-3.5 h, plus a few minutes of scoring.

## Caveats

- **Out of domain.** LingoQA is Wayve UK footage.
- **Single camera.** Only the front camera is used, as in A1.5. A2's VQA profile is 6 cameras, which LingoQA does not have.
- **Judge bias.** Lingo-Judge over-rates long answers (paper App. E). A2 answers that are longer or shorter than A1.5's shift absolute numbers. Arm-to-arm deltas are the readout.

## Confirmatory replication on LingoQA train (pre-registered 2026-11-14, before any train answer was generated)

- **Why.** The only val result where MoOP beats both halves is u50: +8.2 pp vs FM-only (p 0.0012) and +4.4 pp vs CE-only (p 0.036).
  The CE-only comparison is borderline after correcting for the 3 allocations looked at. The goal check ruled it "not clearly
  satisfied", so it gets an independent, larger test.
- **Data.** A fixed random sample of LingoQA **train**: 400 of 3,508 segments × 5 questions = 2,000 questions, seed 20261114,
  `run_a2_lingoqa.py --split train`. There is one human reference per question.
  - The draw is deterministic (checked on CPU), and the sample is saved to each run's `manifest.json`.
  - A2's importance (calib_100 driving clips) never saw LingoQA.
  - Whether A2 was trained on LingoQA train is unknown. That can lift absolute accuracy for every arm, but not a paired comparison.
- **Protocol.** Identical to val: front camera × 4 frames, A2 VQA prompt, greedy, max 128 tokens, Lingo-Judge (logit > 0).
- **Hypotheses (primary family, Holm-corrected, two-sided α = 0.05).**
  - H1: MoOP u50 > FM-only u50.
  - H2: MoOP u50 > CE-only u50.
  - Paired per question; CIs and p come from a bootstrap over the 400 segments (200k draws).
  - The claim "MoOP beats both halves on language" is confirmed only if **both** Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 with positive deltas.
- **Secondary (uncorrected, reported regardless).** u50-q8 MoOP vs FM-only / CE-only; dense.
- **Compute.** cvlab21 Ada 4-7 (2 pairs); Blackwell 0-3 are in use by another user.
  - Arm order: moop_u50, fm_only_u50, ce_only_u50, dense, then the q8 three.
  - About 2,000 × ~2 s per arm, so the primary three take about 2.2 h.
- **Code.** `run_a2_lingoqa.py --split train`, `run_a2_lingoqa_queue.sh` with `SPLIT=train`, `score_a2_lingo_train.py`
  (scoring + the test above).
- **Outputs.** `outputs/iclr27-rebuttal/a2_lingotrain_<arm>/`, `outputs/iclr27-rebuttal/a2_lingotrain_analysis/`.

- **Hypotheses (primary, Holm over the two, two-sided α = 0.05).**
  - H1: MoOP u50-q8 > FM-only u50-q8.
  - H2: MoOP u50-q8 > CE-only u50-q8.
  - Paired per question, segment bootstrap with 200k draws.
  - **Confirmed** only if both Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 with positive deltas.
- **Arms.** MoOP, FM-only and CE-only at u50-q8 only. Hardware: cvlab21 Ada 4-7, about 1.7 h.
