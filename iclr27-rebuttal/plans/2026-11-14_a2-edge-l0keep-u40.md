## Masks

`make_edge_moop_masks.py --l0keep` applies make_slim's l0keep rule to all three criteria:
- Layer 0 keeps its 16 Q heads. Layers 1–27 cut 6 heads, as before.
- The 6 restored heads (6 x 524,288 parameters) are paid back as **768 extra MLP channels**, taken from each layer's own
  ranking: 28 per layer in layers 0–11, 27 in layers 12–27.
- Edge's MLP channel costs 2 x hidden (relu^2). `make_a2_moop_masks`' l0keep branch assumes SwiGLU's 3 x hidden, so the
  rule is re-implemented with the cost as a parameter.
- Gates:
  - G0l: with 3 x hidden, the re-implementation reproduces `make_a2_moop_masks` l0keep masks on A1.5 for
    dual / traj / coc unit for unit.
  - G0p: every l0keep mask removes exactly the plain arm's 509,329,408 parameters (short 0).
  - G0e: per-layer counts are as above.
- All three gates passed.
