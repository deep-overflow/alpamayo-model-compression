# Plan: pre-registered multi-objective test — u50-q8 MoOP vs FM-only and CE-only on fresh driving + language data

Status: pre-registered 2026-11-14 ~19:45 KST, before any of the data below was generated; running. User goal (/goal): "MoOP가
FM-only와 CE-only를 이길 때까지 해줘."

## Why

- **No single-metric win so far.** A2 has no single metric on which MoOP beats both halves (`reports/2026-11-14_a2-moop-vs-halves.md`, final summary).
  - Each half keeps its own objective: FM-only keeps driving, CE-only keeps language.
- **What u50-q8 already shows.** MoOP is never significantly worse than either half there:
  - it beats FM-only on language (replicated 3×, the last one pre-registered);
  - it beats CE-only on driving (OOD-Val −0.348*; u55-q8 pooled −0.222*).
- **The claim being tested.** MoOP is a *multi-objective* method, so the claim matching its purpose is: "MoOP beats each half — better on one objective, not worse on the other."
- **This test.** That claim is fixed here and tested once, on data no q8 arm has ever been evaluated on.

## Data (fresh)

- **Driving.** OOD-train1271: q8 arms were never run on it (only u40 on GH200).
  - Hardware: cvlab21 Blackwell 0-3, `run_a2_arms.sh` with `SET=oodtrain EXP_SUFFIX=_bw`, 2 shards.
  - Arms: `moop_u50_q8`, `fm_only_u50_q8`, `ce_only_u50_q8`.
- **Language.** LingoQA train sample 3: 400 segments × 5 questions, seed 20261116.
  - It is drawn only from segments outside samples 1 and 2 (800 excluded, overlap 0, checked on CPU).
  - Queue: `run_a2_lingoqa_queue.sh` with `SPLIT=train3`, Blackwell pairs.

## Criteria (95% CIs, 200k bootstrap; driving resamples clips, language resamples segments)

| id | objective | comparison | passes if |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-FM | driving minADE@6 6.4 s | MoOP − FM-only | upper bound < +0.05 m (non-inferior) |
| L-FM | LingoQA accuracy | MoOP − FM-only | lower bound > 0 (superior) |
| D-CE | driving minADE@6 6.4 s | MoOP − CE-only | upper bound < 0 (superior) |
| L-CE | LingoQA accuracy | MoOP − CE-only | lower bound > −2.0 pp (non-inferior) |

- **Decision rule.** The claim is confirmed only if **all four** pass. This is an intersection-union test, so no multiplicity correction is needed.
- **Margins, fixed now.**
  - +0.05 m is ≈ 5% of minADE (≈ 1 m) and the arm-to-arm floor for separately measured arms (memory, importance-seed noise).
  - −2 pp is ≈ a quarter of the dense→MoOP LingoQA drop (≈ 8 pp on val).
- **Reported regardless of the verdict.** CoC degeneracy, 1.6 / 3.2 s.
