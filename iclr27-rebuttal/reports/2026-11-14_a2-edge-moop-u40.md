# Experiment: MoOP vs FM-only vs CE-only on Alpamayo 2 Edge at u40 (LingoQA, Test500, OOD-Val250)

2026-11-14. Plan: `iclr27-rebuttal/plans/2026-11-14_a2-edge-moop-u40.md`. User ask: compare MoOP, CE-only and FM-only on
Alpamayo 2 Edge on LingoQA, Test500 and OOD-Val, starting with u40.

## Purpose

- **H1 (driving).** Does MoOP drive better than FM-only and CE-only in open loop on Test500 and OOD-Val?
- **H2 (language).** On LingoQA, does MoOP keep more language ability than FM-only, and as much as CE-only?
- Every arm is also compared with dense Edge.

Not pre-registered (first u40 look, as for A2 Super on 2026-11-09).

## Setup

**Model.** `nvidia/Alpamayo2-Edge@f8951d0`.
- 2.65 B parameters, Cosmos3-Edge (Nemotron-based text tower).
- 28 VLM layers, each with 16 Q heads and 9,216 relu^2 MLP channels.

**Importance.** `edge_importance_v1`: calib_100 (100 clips, t0 5.1 s), no-nav prompt, dual objective
(`reports/2026-11-14_a2-edge-analysis-replication.md`).

**Masks.** `code/make_edge_moop_masks.py` (outputs in `outputs/iclr27-rebuttal/edge_moop/`), which imports
`make_a2_moop_masks.moop_masks` unchanged.
- Scores:
  - MoOP = max of the within-layer rank-normalised I_FM and I_CE
  - FM-only = rank-normalised I_FM alone
  - CE-only = rank-normalised I_CE alone
- u40 = make_slim's ratio 0.3985632694 in every layer: **6 of 16 Q heads and 3,673 of 9,216 MLP channels removed per
  layer.**
- Removed: **509.3 M parameters = 19.2 % of the 2.65 B**, or 36.1 % of the VLM text decoder layers.
- Untouched: the expert, the KV heads, the vision tower and the embeddings.
- G0: the imported function reproduces A1.5's shipped dual / dual-l0keep / traj / coc u40 kept sets unit for unit.
- Each arm removes exactly 6 heads and 3,673 channels in every layer.
- Overlap of the removed sets (share of the first arm's removed units that the second also removes):

  | pair | Q heads | MLP channels |
  |---|---|---|
  | MoOP / FM-only | 0.68 | 0.86 |
  | MoOP / CE-only | 0.74 | 0.88 |
  | FM-only / CE-only | 0.51 | 0.79 |

- Layer 0 Q heads removed:

  | arm | layer-0 heads removed |
  |---|---|
  | MoOP | 0, 2, 3, 12, 14, 15 |
  | FM-only | 0, 2, 3, 12, 14, 15 (same as MoOP) |
  | CE-only | 0, 1, 2, 3, 14, 15 |

**Masks at run time.** Forward pre-hooks that zero the masked inputs of `o_proj` (per Q head) and `mlp.fc2` (per
channel): `code/edge_eval_lib.EdgeVlmMasks`.
- G1, open loop (3 clips) and LingoQA (3 questions):
  - all-ones hooks are bitwise equal to no hooks
  - the masks change the output

**Open loop.** `code/run_edge_openloop.py`, the A2 protocol on Edge:
- One CoC rollout: T 0.6, top_p 0.98, max 256, seed `sha256("42:clip")[:4]`, Edge's own generation path, no-nav prompt.
- Then 8 denoisings (10 Euler steps, seed base + k) on that rollout's KV cache.
- Metric: minADE@6 / minFDE@6 = the minimum over the first 6 of the 8 samples, at 1.6 / 3.2 / 6.4 s.
- Clip sets:
  - Test500: 500 clips.
  - OOD-Val250: the 250 of OOD-Val262 with t0 >= 2.0 s. Edge needs a front frame at t0 − 2 s; user decision. Absolute
    values are therefore not comparable with OOD-Val262 tables.
- Inputs: new Edge caches `test500_edge` / `oodval_edge`, streamed through Edge's loader. The GT future and the last 16
  history poses equal A2's `test500_a2` / `ood_a2` exactly for 500/500 and 250/250 clips.

**LingoQA.** `code/run_edge_lingoqa.py`: the 500 val questions (100 segments × 5).
- Prompt: Edge's native VQA conversation with the front camera only:
  - the last 4 of 5 frames
  - time labels −3 / −2 / −1 / 0 s (LingoQA: 4-s clip at 1 Hz; the spacing is inferred)
- Decoding: greedy, max 128 tokens.
- Scoring: Lingo-Judge (`experiments/lingoqa/score_lingo_vqa.py`).
- Empty answers (MoOP 1, FM-only 4; 3-token outputs that decode to "") are scored as given, which means wrong.

**Statistics.** `code/analyze_edge_u40.py`, using the machinery of `ci_openloop_lingoqa.py`:
- 200,000 bootstrap draws, seed 0, shared draws across arms. The seed-1 reruns agree to ±0.001.
- Open loop: clip-level paired CIs. LingoQA: CIs resample the 100 segments.
- \* marks a 95 % CI that excludes 0.

**Hardware.** All arms and sets on Ada RTX 5880, cards 4–7 (user decision; verified in every run's config).
- At most 2 jobs per card, scheduled by `code/edge_queue.py`.
- 4 CPU threads per job. The default 64-thread torch pools had pushed the box to load 124; see "Execution notes".
- LingoQA runs that had started on Blackwell were stopped and are not used (`edge_lingo_*_bw_aborted`).

## Analysis (Opinion)

- **H2 holds on Edge, and more clearly than on A2 Super.**
  - At u40, FM-only loses 10.6 pp of LingoQA. MoOP and CE-only stay within 2 pp of dense.
  - MoOP − FM-only is +8.8 pp and clearly significant. This is the paper's language result (MoOP keeps what the CE half
    keeps) reproduced on a non-Qwen3-VL model. On A2 Super at u40, no LingoQA difference was significant.
  - It fits the Edge importance analysis. On Edge, I_FM and I_CE already disagree on Q heads in early layers (pre-step
    ρ 0.49), and FM-only and CE-only share only 51 % of their removed Q heads. A selection that ignores I_CE removes
    heads the language side needs.
- **H1 does not hold: MoOP drives no better than either half, and numerically worst.**
  - At 6.4 s, MoOP − FM-only is +0.047 m on Test500 (n.s.) and +0.101 m on OOD-Val250. The OOD CI is [−0.003, +0.216],
    p 0.057, and the seed-1 rerun agrees.
  - MoOP − CE-only is +0.061 / +0.076 m (n.s.).
  - At 3.2 s on OOD-Val, MoOP's FDE is significantly worse than both halves (+0.09 m).
  - All three arms cost 12–22 % at 6.4 s.
  - So on Edge u40, MoOP trades some driving for language: it is CE-only-like in language and no better than FM-only
    in driving. That is the A2 Super u50 pattern (MoOP worse than FM-only, +0.077\*), not the A1.5 one.
- **Where the driving gap comes from.** These are inferences, not tested here.
  - MoOP's removed Q heads are closer to CE-only's (overlap 0.74) than to FM-only's (0.68).
  - The bucket table puts MoOP's extra error mainly in **cruise**:
    - Test500 cruise: MoOP 0.928 vs FM-only 0.851 / CE-only 0.841
    - OOD cruise: 1.012 vs 0.876 / 0.938
  - Cruise is the bucket where the A1.5 layer-0 lesson located selection-driven collapses.
  - MoOP and FM-only remove the same layer-0 heads. So if it is a Q-head effect, it sits in the deeper layers.
  - On A2 Super, q8 (cutting 8 heads and paying with MLP channels) removed MoOP's u50 driving loss. With 16 heads per
    layer, Edge's 6 head cuts are 37.5 % of each layer's heads, so the same lever is the obvious next test.
- **Adaptive reasoning breaks under every pruning criterion.**
  - Dense Edge writes `<NOMINAL>` on 49 % of Test500 clips. The pruned arms almost never do (MoOP 8 %, the halves 0 %);
    they write 24–34-token reasoning instead.
  - Whether this costs driving is not isolated here.
  - It matters for latency: the CoC is 1.6–2.4× longer, which offsets part of the compute saved by pruning.
- **Caveats.**
  - The prompt has no navigation line, which is out of Edge's training distribution (user decision for both the
    importance and the evaluation).
  - OOD-Val is the 250-clip subset.
  - Not pre-registered.
  - The OOD MoOP − FM-only gap is borderline. It needs a confirmatory set (e.g. OOD-train) before it is stated as a loss.
- **Suggested next steps.**
  - u50 as planned by the user ("u40부터").
  - A q-cut variant at the same budget: fewer Q heads cut, more MLP channels.
  - A layer-0 keep arm to test the cruise hypothesis.
  - A confirmatory driving set for MoOP vs FM-only.

## Execution notes

- **Hardware.** Cards changed several times at the user's request: 0–3, then 2–3, then 4–7 only.
  - The 3-clip G1 gates ran on Blackwell. They only check hook correctness.
  - Every reported number is from Ada 4–7.
- **CPU.** The first Ada launch oversubscribed the CPU. Each process opened a 64-thread torch pool, load reached 124 on
  64 cores, and the GPUs idled at 0–54 %.
  - With 4 threads per job, GPU utilisation went to 83–99 % and LingoQA ran 3–4× faster.
  - Runs are deterministic and resumable, so the restart does not change any number.
