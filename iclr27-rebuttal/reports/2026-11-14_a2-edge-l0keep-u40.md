# Experiment: Alpamayo 2 Edge u40 with l0keep -- MoOP / FM-only / CE-only on Test500

2026-11-14 (runs 02:11–02:31 KST, 11-15). Plan: `iclr27-rebuttal/plans/2026-11-14_a2-edge-l0keep-u40.md`. Follow-up to
`reports/2026-11-14_a2-edge-moop-u40.md`.

## Purpose

Does keeping all of layer 0's Q heads (make_slim's l0keep) change the u40 three-way comparison on Edge?

At u40 without l0keep, MoOP drove no better than FM-only or CE-only, and its extra error sat mostly in the cruise bucket.
On A1.5, cruise is where layer-0 Q-head choice caused collapses.

## Setup

**Model, importance, protocol, hardware.** Unchanged from the u40 run:
- `nvidia/Alpamayo2-Edge@f8951d0`, calib_100 importance, no-nav prompt.
- One CoC rollout + 8 denoisings; minADE@6 / minFDE@6 at 1.6 / 3.2 / 6.4 s.
- Ada RTX 5880 cards 4–7, empty cards only, 4 CPU threads per job.
- Only Test500 (500 clips), as asked.

**l0keep masks** (`make_edge_moop_masks.py --l0keep`), the same rule for all three criteria:
- Layer 0 keeps its 16 Q heads. Layers 1–27 cut exactly the same 6 heads as the plain arm (verified identical).
- The 6 restored layer-0 heads (3,145,728 parameters) are paid back as **768 extra MLP channels**:
  - 28 per layer in layers 0–11, 27 per layer in layers 12–27
  - taken from each layer's own ranking
  - a superset of the plain arm's removed channels (verified nested)
- l0keep − plain therefore isolates two things: +6 heads kept in layer 0, and 768 more MLP channels removed.
- Removed parameters: **509,329,408 in every arm, identical to the plain arms** (19.2 % of 2.65 B).
- Edge's MLP channel costs 2 x hidden (relu^2). `make_a2_moop_masks`' l0keep branch assumes 3 x hidden, so the rule is
  re-implemented with the cost as a parameter.
- Gates:
  - G0l: with 3 x hidden, the re-implementation reproduces `make_a2_moop_masks`' l0keep masks on A1.5 for
    dual / traj / coc unit for unit.
  - G0p: exact parameter parity.
  - G0e: per-layer counts are as above.
- Before l0keep, MoOP and FM-only removed the same layer-0 heads (0, 2, 3, 12, 14, 15). CE-only removed 0, 1, 2, 3, 14, 15.

**Reused rows.** Dense and the three plain arms come from the u40 run, on Ada.
- Reproducibility gate: the first 3 Test500 clips of plain MoOP were rerun on card 7. Trajectories, ADE/FDE at every
  horizon and the CoC text are bitwise equal to the stored rows.

**Runs.** `edge_test500_{moop,fm_only,ce_only}_l0keep`, 4 shards each, `edge_queue.py`.
- The queue waited 10 min until another member's jobs left cards 4–7.
- 12/12 jobs succeeded, 500/500 clips per arm.
- Analysis: `analyze_edge_u40.py --preset l0keep`. Bootstrap: 200,000 draws, shared across arms; the seed-1 reruns agree
  to ±0.001.

## Analysis (Opinion)

- **l0keep does not rescue MoOP on Edge.**
  - MoOP-l0keep − MoOP is +0.001 m at 6.4 s, with a CI of ±0.06.
  - The cruise bucket, where the l0keep hypothesis expected the gain, gets worse for MoOP (0.928 → 0.984). Its
    accel / decel / turn buckets improve.
  - So MoOP's u40 driving gap is not a layer-0 head-choice effect. That fits the fact that MoOP and FM-only already
    removed the same layer-0 heads.
  - Same conclusion as on A2 Super (l0keep no effect) and unlike A1.5, where the layer-0 choice drove cruise collapses.
- **The criteria respond differently, and the ranking among the halves flips.**
  - FM-only gains slightly: −0.024 m ADE (n.s.) and −0.107 m FDE\* at 6.4 s. Turn and decel_stop gain most.
  - CE-only loses: +0.047 m\* ADE and +0.116 m\* FDE.
  - Both differ from their plain arm only by +6 layer-0 heads and −768 MLP channels taken by their own ranking. So the
    cost of the extra MLP cut is criterion-dependent: CE's MLP ranking appears to protect fewer driving-relevant
    channels at the margin.
  - The median per-clip effects are small (−0.001 / +0.008 m). The mean shifts come from a minority of clips.
  - FM-only-l0keep also gets some `<NOMINAL>` back (12 % vs 0 %). This is the only pruned arm that partly keeps Edge's
    adaptive reasoning.
- **The three-way comparison under l0keep.**
  - FM-only-l0keep is the best pruned driver (1.022, +11.6 % vs dense).
  - MoOP-l0keep and CE-only-l0keep tie (1.095 / 1.080).
  - None of the pairwise differences is significant at 6.4 s.
  - MoOP-l0keep − FM-only-l0keep widens to +0.072 m (p 0.14) from +0.047 m without l0keep. It stays n.s., and the 1.6 s
    gap is small but significant (+0.007 m\*).
  - So with or without l0keep, on Edge u40 MoOP does not beat either half on driving.
- **Recommendation.**
  - Keep the plain u40 arms as the primary Edge comparison: l0keep changes nothing for MoOP.
  - If the paper uses l0keep as the default recipe, report the Edge l0keep rows as the matching variant. The MoOP
    conclusion does not change.
  - The remaining driving lever worth testing is the head budget. On A2 Super, cutting fewer Q heads per layer at the
    same parameter budget (q8 at u50) removed MoOP's driving loss. On Edge, a q-cut variant at u40 is the analogous test.
  - LingoQA and OOD-Val for the l0keep arms were not run (Test500 only, as asked).
- **Caveats.**
  - Not pre-registered.
  - Test500 only.
  - No-nav prompt.
  - The FM-only FDE gain (CI −0.21 to −0.008) is borderline-sized and needs a second set before it is relied on.
