## Panels and caption notes

- `fig1_depth_q_head`, `fig1_depth_mlp`: objective importance summed over
  units in each VLM layer, then independently normalized to each objective's
  maximum. Blue solid: trajectory FM; orange dashed: reasoning NLL.
  Layer indices are zero-based; vertical lines identify maxima.
- `fig1_rank_agreement`: within-layer Spearman correlation between the two
  objectives. Shading identifies layers 22–35. Constant-score layers have
  undefined correlations and are omitted.
- `fig1_kept_overlap`: intersection size divided by retained-set size,
  keeping 19/32 heads or 7390/12288 MLP channels. Horizontal references are
  the expected overlap of independent uniformly selected sets of those sizes.
  Shading identifies layers 22–35. Constant-score layers are omitted because
  their top-k sets are arbitrary; consequently late-layer averages exclude
  layer 35 (Q: 0.676; MLP: 0.688).
  Caption guard: overlap answers whether the two objectives disagree, not what
  a wrong choice costs -- it does not order arms by damage. On this same
  budget dualsafe retains 94.0% of dual's Q heads and costs +0.1945 m on
  val500, while maxstep11 displaces 7.0-9.7% for -0.0033 m. Do not caption
  this panel with a claim about the size of a criterion's effect.
- `fig2_steps_q_head`, `fig2_steps_mlp`: Spearman agreement between denoising
  steps, averaged over expert layers. Both panels use the same 0–1 color
  scale. Off-diagonal summary statistics exclude self-correlation and are reported here,
  rather than inside the panels.
  Means: Q 0.668; MLP 0.874. These are descriptive layer averages, not CIs.
- `fig2_mass_curve`: scores summed across steps, sorted within each layer,
  cumulatively normalized within layer, then averaged over layers. The dotted
  line marks a nominal 90% removal budget; markers use the actual discrete
  fraction `floor(0.9 * n_units) / n_units`, so a marker can lie to its left.
  Origin is explicitly included. Mass at that budget: Q 68.5%; MLP 1.8%.
- `fig2_stability_vs_mass`: one point per expert layer; mean pairwise step
  agreement versus importance in the lowest-ranked `floor(0.9 * n_units)`
  units. The vertical axis is logarithmic. Circles: Q; triangles: MLP.
  Within-axis Spearman correlations: Q −0.451 (p=0.006), MLP −0.436 (p=0.008).
  These associations do not establish causation.

The 2026-09-08 restyling retains the input data and score definitions, adds
redundant line/marker encoding, and fixes undefined retained-set overlap,
clipped heatmap values, and discrete-budget annotation positions.

## Text placement

Panel titles, peak labels, expanded objective names, random-baseline text, and
correlation summaries are left to the manuscript caption. Axis labels and
series legends remain; the mass-curve percentages remain as the central
quantitative comparison. Identify Q-head and MLP depth/heatmap panels in the
subcaptions. All layer and denoising-step indices are zero-based. Depth peaks
are Q: trajectory 19 / CoC 24, MLP: trajectory 18 / CoC 27.

Typography uses DejaVu Sans with upright DejaVu Sans math, including Greek
symbols and logarithmic tick labels. Subscripts retain their mathematical
placement. PDF embeds TrueType fonts; SVG stores glyph outlines so viewing
does not depend on fonts installed on the reader's computer.

## Figure 3 panels: why the two objectives differ

Generated by `experiments/paper/fig_why_differs.py` from stored runs only (CPU, about
two minutes). Same panel size, fonts and export formats as above; every number quoted
below is written to `fig3_why_differs_stats.json` by the same run.

```bash
MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp/alpamayo-mpl .venv/bin/python experiments/paper/fig_why_differs.py
```

Inputs: `outputs/importance_v2/{importance,importance_perclip}.npz`,
`outputs/importance_vqa`, `outputs/importance_dim0`, `outputs/importance_dim1`,
`outputs/jlens_v2/jlens.npz`, and twelve other importance runs for the robustness panel
(`DRAWS` in the script). Layer 35 is excluded throughout because the trajectory score is
identically zero there.

- `fig3_ratio_step`: raw layer sums, `I_traj / I_CoC`, log axis. Dotted segments are the
  two levels of a least-squares two-level fit. Change point: layer 23 is the first layer
  of the lower level on both axes (Q 14.4x -> 3.1x, MLP 12.7x -> 2.8x). Step 4.64x
  [3.85, 5.47] (Q) and 4.56x [3.37, 6.07] (MLP), bootstrap over clips; the single step
  explains R2 = 0.86 / 0.93 of the log-ratio's variation over depth. The step is present
  in 100% / 99% of individual clips.
  Caption guard: the two losses are in different units, so the LEVELS (14x, 3x) carry no
  meaning -- only the flat-step-flat shape does. Do not write "the trajectory loss is
  14 times more sensitive".
- `fig3_ratio_step_draws` (supplementary): the MLP ratio of twelve other runs, each
  divided by its own layer 0-21 mean. Disjoint calibration draws, n = 100 / 500 / 4000,
  OOD clips, a self-anchored trajectory target, acceleration-only and curvature-only. The
  change point is 23 on the Q axis in all twelve, and 23 (eleven) or 24 (curvature-only)
  on the MLP axis.
- `fig3_noise_ceiling_q_head`, `fig3_noise_ceiling_mlp`: the calibration clips are split
  into disjoint halves 50 times. Blue / orange: the same objective on the other half --
  the ceiling any agreement can reach at this sample size. Black: the other objective on
  the other half. Q heads: ceiling flat in depth (traj 0.85 -> 0.82, CoC 0.90 -> 0.89),
  black 0.74 -> 0.33. MLP: ceiling falls (0.69 -> 0.46, 0.80 -> 0.50), black 0.69 -> 0.26.
  Agreement corrected for the ceiling: Q 0.85 -> 0.37, MLP 0.92 -> 0.54; three disjoint
  100-clip draws measured as separate runs give 0.85 -> 0.37 and 0.90 -> 0.53. The
  falling ceiling accounts for 3% of the multiplicative fall in agreement for Q heads
  and 34% for MLP channels at n = 100 (44% at n = 50).
  Caption guard: these are half-sample (n = 50) reliabilities, lower than the full-sample
  ones, and the correction is the Pearson attenuation formula applied to Spearman values.
- `fig3_partition_q_head`, `fig3_partition_mlp`: means of three single-clip Spearman
  correlations per layer -- other clip and other objective (shared), same clip and other
  objective minus shared (clip-specific), other clip and same objective minus shared
  (objective-specific, averaged over the two objectives). The MLP panel uses a log axis
  because layers 1-5 sit at 0.6-0.8; every plotted value is positive. The unplotted
  remainder (55-82%) is single-clip noise plus the clip x objective interaction, which
  one measurement per clip cannot separate.
- `fig3_boundary`: three signals, each min-max normalised over layers 0-34. Importance
  ratio = log of the MLP curve in `fig3_ratio_step`. FM cache sensitivity = the stored
  `traj_kv_k + traj_kv_v` summed over KV groups: 81% of it lies at or below layer 22 and
  its layer 8-22 mean is 3.6x its layer 25-35 mean. Lens kurtosis = excess kurtosis of the
  Jacobian-lens readout over random vocabulary tokens (`jlens_v2`), 6.4 over layers 14-20
  and 21.0 over 24-34, two-level change point 22. Shading marks layers 21-24.
  Caption guard: the lens is measured at text and CoC positions only, so the kurtosis
  describes the text-position stream, not the vision-position one.
- `fig3_text_write_q_head`, `fig3_text_write_mlp`: Spearman correlation between each
  objective's score and the unit's write norm over text and CoC positions (`q_w`, `mlp_w`
  in `jlens_v2`; vision, history and sink excluded). Q heads, layers 6-21 -> 22-34:
  CoC 0.50 -> 0.77, trajectory 0.24 -> 0.25. MLP: CoC 0.48 -> 0.44, trajectory
  0.23 -> 0.30 -- the contrast is a Q-head result.
- `fig3_pairs_q_head`, `fig3_pairs_mlp`: within-layer Spearman agreement for four pairs of
  scores. Layers 6-21 -> 22-34, Q heads: CoC vs CoC on LingoQA images 0.80 -> 0.87,
  acceleration vs curvature 0.79 -> 0.67, VQA vs CoC on the same images 0.57 -> 0.42,
  trajectory vs CoC 0.77 -> 0.33. MLP: 0.74 -> 0.50, 0.55 -> 0.36, 0.63 -> 0.27,
  0.68 -> 0.34.
  Caption guard: on the MLP axis every pair falls late, including two halves of the same
  FM loss, so do not read the MLP panel as evidence of objective-specific units without
  the noise-ceiling panel beside it.
